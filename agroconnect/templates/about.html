🌏 Connecting farmers directly to buyers  •  🌿 Farm-gate prices  •  ⚡ Zero registration friction for farmers

Our Story

AgroConnect started with a conversation between three friends who kept asking the same question: why does a bag of tomatoes cost ₦800 at the farm gate but ₦3,500 at the market? The answer — unnecessary middlemen, information gaps, and zero trust infrastructure.

In 2024, Ayomide Awoniyi, Kizito Adaka, and Israel Babalola set out to build a platform that gives every farmer — even one who has never owned a smartphone — the tools to reach buyers directly, negotiate fairly, and get paid what their harvest is worth.

Today, AgroConnect supports anonymous guest listings, real-time price broadcasts, group-buy pooling, Paystack-powered payments, and SMS notifications that work even on feature phones. We're just getting started.

What We Stand For

⚖
Farmer First
Every decision we make starts with one question — does this make life better for the farmer? Zero registration fees, anonymous listings, SMS fallback. Always.
🔑
Radical Transparency
Farm-gate prices are public. Negotiation threads are visible to both parties. No hidden fees. No surprises. Trust is our most valuable product.
🌎
Built for Nigeria
SMS notifications for feature phones. State and LGA filters for all 36 states. Naira pricing. Harvest calendars by geo-zone. This isn't a Western product retrofitted for Africa.
📈
Fair Economics
Group-buy pooling lets small buyers match wholesale quantities. Price broadcasts let buyers post their needs. The market works for everyone, not just the biggest players.
⚡
Speed & Simplicity
Post a listing in under 2 minutes. No account needed. Reference code protects your listing. Freshness scores build trust automatically over time.
🅣
Seasonal Intelligence
Our harvest calendar and season signals help buyers and farmers plan around nature — not fight it. Buy in-season, pay less, waste less.
